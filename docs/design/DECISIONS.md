| — | Presentation architecture (binding, added 2026-07-28) | **The tile simulation is the model; every camera is a view over it.** Eli, emphatic and unprompted: *"do not ditch the tile sim... we need the tile sim in the background even if the player playing through it sees something 1st person."* Long-range target register (POST-simulation, not current work): *"Morrowind skills and systems with FO New Vegas factions with Daggerfall's graphics"* + *"faked 3D via clever use of 2D as Gabri sleuths about"* — where "Daggerfall's graphics" means its **billboarded sprites for everything alive**, NOT its raycast geometry. Why this is architecturally cheap rather than a rewrite: ArchUnit already forbids `sim-core` importing libGDX/`client-observer`, so the sim does not know it is being drawn; `client-observer` is *a* client, not *the* client, and a first-person combat screen (or a later exploration camera) is another view over the same deterministic tick stream — two views can never disagree, because the sim is the single source of truth. Everything load-bearing is grid logic a camera cannot disturb: 1-occupant-per-cell, A* pathing, z-links, sense radii, work reach (chebyshev 2), the S7 corridor-pinch predicate. `Actor.facing()` already exists (it prints on the character sheet) and Play mode already tracks a played actor, so a soul's-eye camera is largely position + facing. Depth Vision (S4, extended to actors in S5-6) already renders lower z-bands with scaled darkening and cool haze — a real working depth cue, and most of the way to convincing pseudo-3D with no polygons. **Rules that bind any future camera work:** (1) it is a NEW CLIENT MODULE or a client-side screen, NEVER a sim change; (2) the headless tile view stays the verification source of truth, so no rendered scene can quietly disagree with the sim; (3) it must not touch the S8-S12 economy arc — you cannot investigate an economy with no secrets in it, so the world that generates the mysteries gets built first. |

| — | Trojian housing: Compounds | (Eli, 2026-07-13) **Housing is organized into Compounds, not detached houses.** A Compound = one large walled lot: a courtyard/atrium farm at the center (crops for the resident families) surrounded by condo-like apartments or mansions belonging to owning families. Built up multiple stories where land is scarce; the ROOFTOP of a tall compound is walled off and rented out as mass low-income housing (tents/mud huts — informal, flammable, cheap). Wealth gradient IN MATERIALS: mansions/condos of owning families = **Reman engineering** (concrete-equivalent — Rema is the canon engineer-civilization); rooftop slum housing = cheap cloth/mudbrick/tent construction. Visual touchstone Eli gave: "the big crab in Ald-Ruhn" (Morrowind) — one large architectural shell containing a whole community's housing stratified by income, not a suburb of identical single-family lots. **Applies retroactively**: the "12 homes" of the city-block population effort become ~12 dwelling UNITS within one compound (a few condo/mansion units + several rooftop tent/hut units), not 12 separate buildings. **"Skyrunners"**: Granadad's in-world term for burglars/assassins, named because they break in through compound ceilings and flee across the rooftop-slum layer — a natural third `Job.Villain.*` alongside Robber/Cutpurse, tied to the Skyrunning skill and the Persona cover-identity seam. **Rooftops are socially unseemly** — being seen on one marks a Trojian as low-status or criminal; this is *why* the poor live there and *why* it's a villain highway. Retroactively explains BLESSING-QUEUE #6 (Skylining/Skyrunning kept Favored "for the Wielder") — Gabri's maxed social immunity exempts him alone from that stigma. Binding: no future rooftop-social mechanic may penalize a presented-Wielder actor. |
