## The workbench -- the owner's editor (SHIPPED, window unrun)

Asked mid-session: *"make your own simple c++ editor that works like unity where you can
use it to work in and have multiple workflows seeing it."* Clarified with the owner: a
**live world workbench**, built after errands and shops. Guide: `docs/WORKBENCH.md`.

- **`granadad-workbench`**, a second executable (installed beside `granadad.exe`), and
  a window-free library (`granadad-workbench`) the suite links on every build.
- **Six panes over one live `render::Session`**: HIERARCHY (people, places, the board,
  the Kit, filterable), SCENE (the ward from above at the selection's band, every body
  a dot in its trade's colour, the selection ringed, the player a wedge, four zooms),
  GAME (the session's own 640x360 frame, HUD and conversation included), INSPECTOR (the
  sim's facts about the selection: post and whether they are at it, home, coin, who E
  answers, the errands they give or are named in, the counter they keep; a place's
  occupants, the Gull's own cast included), CONSOLE, a command line, and a toolbar.
- **Built from the game's own primitives** -- `drawText`, the ward map's cell classifier
  and viewport -- with no new dependency, so the window and a headless PNG are the
  same picture.
- **Every command is a public game verb** (`stepMany`, `skipToHour`, `placeBodyAt`,
  `interact`, `chooseTopic`, `giveItem` ...). A **scene** is the log of world-changing
  commands; `load` replays it onto a fresh world to the **identical world hash**
  (pinned). Scenes never touch `content/`.
- **Agents**: `--script=FILE`, and `--serve=DIR` -- any number of workflows drop
  `inbox/*.cmd`, answers go to `outbox/`, and `frame.png`, `state.json` and `log.txt`
  are the shared view. Proven with two workflows on the real binary: the SCORER's
  `state` saw the world exactly as the FEATURE's commands left it.

  panel and speech eases so a paused frame shows the conversation just opened. Nothing
