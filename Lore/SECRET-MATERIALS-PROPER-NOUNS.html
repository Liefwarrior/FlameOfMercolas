1. The Beast: Core Entity and Dimensional Prison

The Beast, designated as the Dark One or the 8th in historical records, originated as a human elevated to a higher-dimensional state via ritualistic means. Confined to omnispace—a 10-dimensional construct—this entity underwent a 100,000-year adaptation period, resulting in cognitive distortion and reality-manipulative capabilities. Its influence manifests through Y'marr fragments, which serve as extensions of its fractured heart, promoting corruption and instability across Mercolas.

Key Attributes:

  • Human-derived emotional spectrum, enabling strategic manipulation.

  • Liminal ontology, existing between states of existence.
