Lore Documentation: The Beast and Omnispace

Version: 3.0 | Date: February 03, 2026

In the shadowed annals of Mercolas, where empires crumble and heroes rise from the ashes, lies the tale of the Beast—once a man, now a force that bends reality itself. This document weaves the threads of his existence, drawing from ancient whispers and blood-soaked battles, to reveal how this Dark One intertwines with the fate of all lands.

1. The Beast Unveiled: From Man to Monstrosity

The Beast, known in hushed tones as the Dark One or the 8th, began as a mortal soul thrust into godhood by a cabal seeking to propel humanity beyond its frail limits. Trapped in the void of Oblivion—omnispace, a realm of ten twisting dimensions—he endured a gestation of 100,000 years. This was no gentle birth; it was a forging in chaos, where his human essence fractured under forces unknown, emerging as a being of raw power and fractured sanity. In the chronicles of Lord of Trojia, echoes of this entity resound through the Y’marr, vile fragments of his shattered heart scattered across the continent, corrupting all they touch. These abominations, writhing masses of darkness that possess and devour, are but shards of the Beast's essence, bending bars in their prison as he does in his. His rage, when unleashed, promises not just war, but the unraveling of Mercolas itself—a cataclysm where heroes like Eric must stand against the tide or be swept into oblivion.

Key Traits Forged in the Void:

  • A core of human fury, plotting with the cunning of kings long fallen.
  • Warping of spacetime, erratic as a storm, capable of shattering armies or raising the dead.
  • A liminal existence, neither living nor ended, feeding on the world's fragile balance.

2. The Eternal Gestation: Cycles of Ruin

For eons, the Beast languished in omnispace's womb, his mind assailed by dimensions beyond mortal ken. As he adapted, grasping at threads of reality, Mercolas suffered. Civilizations bloomed only to wither under apocalyptic floods, plagues, and wars—echoes of his turmoil. In Trojia's lore, this mirrors the endless conflicts, where empires like Gunthred's rise on the bones of the fallen, only to face rebellion's blade. The Beast's influence seeped through, birthing the Y’marr as harbingers, their ichor-like forms boiling with his imprisoned wrath. Humanity's history became a grim tapestry of rise and fall, each cycle a scream from the void, until now, when his full awakening threatens to shatter the bars holding him in Oblivion.

  • Cataclysms every few centuries, resetting progress like a divine curse.
  • Sharpened intellect twisted by isolation, perceiving time as a fractured mirror.
  • Subtle manipulations, guiding rebels and tyrants alike toward his escape.

3. Wielding the Void: Influence on Mercolas

As the Beast's grasp strengthened, his shadow fell heavy on the lands. In Vervadia's plains, horselords felt unnatural strength; in Trojia's halls, tyrants wielded minds like weapons. The Y’marr, his heart's pieces, corrupted flesh and stone, turning guardians into monsters. Eric's journey, from orphan to Luxerne, stands as defiance—channeling energy to slay these fragments, delaying the Beast's break from Oblivion. Yet each victory weakens the bars, inviting a rage that could eclipse Gunthred's conquests, turning allies into ashes and heroes into legends forgotten.

Phase Manifestation Tie to Mercolas
Early Torment Overwhelmed senses, wild outbursts. Global calamities mirroring Trojia's unprovoked wars.
Mid Awakening Gaining tendrils of control. Birth of Y’marr, possessing rebels in Mercia's hills.
Imminent Breach Full warping potential. Luxerne's quest to destroy fragments, averting total unraveling.

4. Omnispace: The Prison of Dimensions

Oblivion's heart, omnispace, is a labyrinth of ten dimensions where thoughts warp through geometric hells. The Beast's human form projected upward, skewed at each layer, becoming something vaster yet broken. To return, he must reverse this—crafting actions that pierce back to Mercolas, perhaps as avatars or cataclysms. In the tales, this echoes the Luxerne's energy links, fragile bridges against the void's pull, where one misstep invites the Beast's essence to flood through.

Mortal Deed in 3D → Ascent through Skewed Realms (Geometric Fracture per Dimension) → Omnispace Manifestation
Reversal: Void Thought → Counter Skew → Mercolas Intrusion (Avatars, Y’marr Spawns)

He is the amplified echo of humanity, his escape a grim promise of godhood's price.

5. Echoes in the Abyss: Analogies of Torment

5.1 The Whale's Fall

Like a great beast sinking to abyssal depths, the Beast's mind was devoured by seven unfamiliar forces—each a ravenous entity stripping his sanity until habituation set in. This left him a spectral husk, fueling the Y’marr's corruption as scavengers on his essence.

5.2 The Projected Shadow

A mortal soul cast upon omnispace's wall, distorted yet enduring—his personality a flickering image, amplified in scope but chained in form.

5.3 The Masked Scream

Beneath an alien facade, a human voice cries out—trapped, directing chaos as best it can, much like the Beast's veiled commands through Y’marr and omens.

6. Ambitions from the Void: Paths to Freedom

The Beast craves release, reversing his ascent to reclaim flesh. Foiled, as in the Luxerne's stand, he unleashes mad god fury—remaking Mercolas in Kefka's image, where skies bleed and lands twist. This climax ties to Eric's saga, where slaying Y’marr fragments buys time, but failure invites the end of all.

  • Avatar manifestation: A 3D echo to walk among men.
  • Rage unbound: Worlds fractured, heroes tested in ultimate grimdark trial.

8. The Y'marr: Fragments of the Heart

Shattered pieces of the Beast's corrupted heart, the Y'marr lurk in Mercolas's underbelly—writhing ichor that possesses the living, twisting them into profane vessels. Ancient evils, they scream unearthly howls, boiling with darkness that explodes in profane flames. Slaying one requires Luxerne might, as Eric and allies discovered, but each death weakens Oblivion's bars, hastening the Beast's return.

9. The Luxerne: Bearers of Light

The Luxerne, chosen warriors infused with the seven magics, stand as beacons against the Y'marr's darkness. Each paired with a fragment's corruption, they wield powers to seal the void's leaks, their battles a dance of light and shadow. Stylized as radiant guardians, their essence pulses with ethereal glows, symbolizing hope's fragile flame in Mercolas's grim night.

Eric & Y'marr 1: Energy Weave - Raw power to cleave darkness.

Ignis & Y'marr 2: Flame Dominion - Incinerating shadows with blue fire.

Adelia & Y'marr 3: Air Fury - Winds that scatter corrupted essence.

Hart & Y'marr 4: Earth Resilience - Unbreakable shields against possession.

Jared & Y'marr 5: Mind Veil - Psychic wards repelling mental incursions.
