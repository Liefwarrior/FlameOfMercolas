# The first-person view, reproducible with no hands (ObserverScript tape, --script=).
#
#   ./gradlew.bat :client-observer:run --args="--fixture=docks --smoke=1500 \
#       --script=<abs path>/content/playtests/fpv_tarwalk.txt"
#
# A smoke run advances exactly one sim tick per rendered frame, so a frame number is a tick
# number and two runs of this tape produce the same session and pixel-identical screenshots.
# Every verb below goes through the same apply* seam the live keys call: nothing here is a
# back door, and none of it can be done to the world by a camera.
#
# Two things to know when reading the frame numbers. Animation is timed in WALL-CLOCK
# seconds, and a smoke frame takes a millisecond or two rather than a sixtieth of a second,
# so a cross-fade that covers twenty frames at 60fps eats a hundred or more here. And the
# subject is left to her own legs for the first thousand ticks: Ditta Pilchard (#1) lodges
# inland and works the quay apron, and letting the sim walk her out there is both the honest
# way to reach the waterfront and a demonstration that the camera is not driving anything.

   2 select=1
   3 follow
   6 zoom=4
